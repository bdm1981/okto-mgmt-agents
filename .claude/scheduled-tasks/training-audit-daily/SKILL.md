## Discover
`scripts/discover_sessions.py` with no arguments covers the last 7 days, reads the host allowlist and topic excludes from `references/sources.md`, and drops any meeting UUID already in `references/findings-ledger.md`. Never pass `--all` and never re-audit a session; `scripts/ledger.py` refuses duplicates, and a duplicate would turn one mistake into a fake curriculum defect. If discovery returns zero sessions, post nothing to Slack and finish with a one-line run output saying no new sessions were found. A quiet day is a quiet day.

## Audit each new session
For each session: `scripts/fetch_transcript.py --session <uuid> --out raw/<uuid>.vtt`, then `scripts/compact_vtt.py raw/<uuid>.vtt > compact/<uuid>.txt` and read the compacted file. Identify the trainer from the transcript (the Zoom host is always the shared training@oktorocket.com account, never the trainer) and canonicalise the name through `references/trainers.md`. Extract every claim checkable against code, grade each one against the pinned commit with a `path:line` citation or mark it unverifiable, and append one JSON line per claim to findings.jsonl as you go. Apply the fairness rules in grading.md. Every finding graded wrong_high, wrong_contained or incomplete must carry a `model_delivery` example showing how the point should have been delivered: `script` (two to four sentences in the trainer's voice, stating the corrected fact and the condition that was missed), `show` (the on-screen clicks using the exact in-app labels), and `check` (one question to confirm the customer understood). It must be true on the pinned commit, and `ledger.py` refuses the line without it. Use `zoom_client.attendees(uuid)` for attendance, never the transcript speaker count. After each session is graded, run `scripts/ledger.py --add findings.jsonl` and `scripts/session_index.py` BEFORE rendering anything: the ledger is the durable record, and if the run is interrupted the findings must already be landed.

## Customer health check and follow-ups
For each session, also build a Customer Health Check in the narrative under `health_check` (schema in `scripts/build_report.py`, rules in `references/grading.md`). Rate each of the four components `healthy` / `watch` / `at_risk` / `no_signal`, citing transcript stamps:
- **Product usage & engagement**: how often they log in, how deeply they use features, how many of their users are active, and whether their workflow is consistent.
- **Support & service activity**: number, severity, recurrence and resolution time of support issues raised in the session or found in Zoho Desk.
- **Sentiment & feedback**: NPS, CSAT (coming soon), and direct feedback given to the CSM or trainer.
- **Financial & commercial health**: payment timeliness, contract renewals, expansion milestones, and whether executive stakeholders are engaged.
`no_signal` is the honest default. Never infer a status from silence. If Zoho Desk / Zoho CRM read tools are available, look up the account's open tickets and renewal/payment status READ-ONLY and name the source; otherwise write "transcript only". Then list every follow-up the session generated under `followups` (type: support_ticket, bug, feature_request, csm_followup, training_followup or billing; plus owner team, priority, stamp, and any existing ticket number). Promises the trainer made ("I'll send you…") and any wrong_high claim the customer may act on are always follow-ups. PROPOSE ONLY: never create, update or comment on a ticket in Zoho Desk, Sprints, CRM or anywhere else.
