## Identifying the trainer

1. **Not from the Zoom host.** Every session runs through the shared `training@oktorocket.com`
   account, so the host is always "OktoRocket Training".
2. **From the transcript's own introduction** — "my name is …", usually in the first two minutes.
3. **When the name is never stated** (5 of 15 August Foundations runs), do not guess from writing
   style. Record `unidentified` and say so in the report's caveats. A wrong attribution is worse
   than a missing one: it invents a cross-trainer defect or hides a real one.
